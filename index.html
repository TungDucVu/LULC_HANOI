1. Composite Generation & Resampling

Uses dry-season (Jan 1 - Feb 15) Sentinel-2 Level-2A surface reflectance composites. Bicubic resampling is forced directly on individual tiles to preserve a strict 10-meter cell size and project accurately without default Earth Engine scaling degradation.

2. Multi-Source Integration

Integrates ALOS DSM (30m elevation and calculated slope) resampled to 10m to add topographical predictors. Features such as GLCM texture contrast (on NIR band) and temporal NDVI standard deviation are engineered to resolve complex urban/agricultural noise.

3. Stratified Automated Sampling & Spectral Filtering

Samples 2,500 training points stratified according to reclassified ESA WorldCover 2021 labels. Applies rigorous physical index filters (e.g., NDVI limits for greenery/bare soil, MNDWI thresholding for water) to eliminate reference label noise. Water profiles are augmented with 50 manually digitized pixels from the Red River.

4. Random Forest Classifier

Trains a smileRandomForest classifier with 200 trees. Post-processing cleans remaining speckle noise using a circular Focal Mode spatial filter (radius 1px) and forces a permanent water mask over the main Red River channel using static ESA labels.

The classifier uses a total of 16 predictor features engineered from spectral bands, spectral indexes, elevation layers, temporal standard deviation, and texture metrics.

Feature Type Name Description / Formula
Spectral Bands B2, B3, B4, B8, B11, B12 Sentinel-2 bands corresponding to Blue, Green, Red, Near-Infrared, and Short-Wave Infrared.
Spectral Indices NDVI, NDBI, MNDWI Classical indices tracking vegetation vitality, built-up surfaces, and open water bodies.
Advanced Indices EVI, SAVI, BSI Enhanced Vegetation, Soil-Adjusted Vegetation, and Bare Soil indices to handle soil background variations.
Topography elevation, slope Elevation and slope calculated from JAXA ALOS AW3D30 digital surface model.
Temporal Variability NDVI_stdDev Standard deviation of NDVI calculated over a full year to identify seasonal crop cycles vs. static urban surfaces.
Texture Features B8_contrast GLCM contrast texture metric calculated on the B8 NIR band to capture spatial pattern differences.

Independent validation performed on a 30% random split of clean reference samples. Overall Accuracy: 83.48%, Kappa: 0.7930.
